About AfterVisit AI

Built to make after-visit instructions easier to understand.

Patients often leave appointments overwhelmed. This demo shows how a calmer, simpler summary and voice-friendly tools can support better follow-through.

Patient-friendly language
Voice support
Hackathon-ready
Browser-only demo

Mission

Reduce confusion after the visit.

AfterVisit AI helps translate rushed, jargon-heavy clinical instructions into something easier to review, share, and remember.

Patients need clarity, not more overwhelm.
Caregivers need quick ways to understand next steps.
Voice features make instructions more accessible.

What this demo includes

Plain-language summaries

Rewrite doctor notes into explanation, actions, warnings, and questions using mock AI rules.

Voice flow

Speech in and speech out

Capture spoken notes in supported browsers and read the final simplified summary back aloud.

Why mock AI

Fast and easy to demo

No backend, no API keys, and no deployment complexity while still showing the end-to-end UX.
