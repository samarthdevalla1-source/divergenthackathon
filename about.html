About AfterVisit AI

Built to make after-visit instructions easier to understand.

Patients often leave appointments overwhelmed. AfterVisit AI shows how a calmer, simpler summary and voice-friendly tools can support better follow-through.

Mission

Reduce confusion after the visit.

AfterVisit AI helps translate rushed, jargon-heavy clinical instructions into something easier to review, share, and remember.

Patients need clarity, not more overwhelm.
Caregivers need quick ways to understand next steps.

What this demo includes

Plain-language summaries

Rewrite doctor notes into explanation, actions, warnings, and questions that are easier to review.

Voice flow

Speech in and speech out
